Week 6 insights

Connecting embedded systems and circuits.

Week 6 focused on understanding computational hardware, embedded systems, and practical electronics.

I explored microcontrollers, sensors, actuators, input/output devices, and the interaction between software and physical hardware.

Computational Hardware & Embedded Systems

Sensing, processing and controlling the physical world.

Embedded systems are dedicated computing systems designed to perform specific functions. They combine hardware and software to sense, process information, and control physical outputs.

03Day 3

Electronic Components & Hardware Instrumentation

Measuring components and verifying circuit behavior.

The practical session introduced basic electronic components and measurement instruments. Resistors control current and provide voltage division; capacitors store electrical energy for filtering, timing, and energy storage; LEDs convert electrical energy into visible light.

  • Resistors, capacitors, LEDs and wires
  • Power supply and circuit connections
  • Multimeter and component measurement
  • Oscilloscope / DSO instrumentation
04Day 4

Soldering & Electronic Assembly

Creating precise electrical and mechanical connections.

The soldering practical session gave me hands-on experience with electronic assembly. I learned how to position components correctly, apply solder to create electrical and mechanical connections, and inspect solder joints for quality.

  • Component placement and handling
  • Soldering technique and joint formation
  • PCB handling and circuit assembly
  • Electrical continuity and safety

05Day 5

555 Timer Circuit & Practical Electronics

Building a timing circuit from components.

The 555 timer practical activity provided hands-on experience in building an electronic circuit using a 555 Timer IC, LEDs, resistors, a capacitor, a power supply, and circuit connections. It introduced the timing concept through practical assembly without relying on undocumented specifications.

Big takeaway

Good engineering connects every layer.

Good engineering combines computational thinking, embedded hardware, and practical electronic skills to create systems that are functional, measurable, and reliable.

Key learnings

What I carried forward.

  • Embedded systems connect software with physical hardware.
  • Sensors provide information from the physical environment.
  • Actuators allow systems to produce physical outputs.
  • Electronic components must be correctly selected and connected.
  • Measurement instruments help verify circuit behavior.
  • Good soldering requires precision and proper component handling.
  • Hardware and software must work together to create complete engineering systems.
