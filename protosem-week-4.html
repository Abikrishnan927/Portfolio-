Big takeaway

Digital fabrication connects imagination to matter.

Digital fabrication creates a direct connection between digital design and real-world manufacturing. Laser cutting and 3D printing helped me understand how different fabrication methods transform digital models into physical components. At the same time, the formation of the Alpha team highlighted the importance of communication, responsibility, and collaboration in successful engineering projects.

Week 4 moved my learning from digital design toward physical manufacturing. I learned that successful fabrication depends not only on the design, but also on machine parameters, material selection, accuracy, and tolerances. The Alpha team formation provided a foundation for upcoming collaborative projects and a shared engineering objective.
