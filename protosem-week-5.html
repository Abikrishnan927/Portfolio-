ProtoSem · Week 5

UI/UX Design, Problem Statements & Project Management

Understanding Users, Defining Problems and Planning Projects

This week focused on understanding the relationship between user experience, problem identification, thoughtful design, and project management. We explored how good interfaces are built around user needs, how a clear problem statement creates direction for innovation, and how project management and mind mapping help organize ideas and project activities.

Week 5 insights

From user needs to organized action.

Week 5 introduced me to the fundamentals of UI/UX design and helped me understand how user needs, usability, and problem identification influence the development of a solution.

We explored the basic principles of UI/UX design and the importance of understanding users before developing a solution. This helped me look beyond the visual appearance of a product and consider how users interact with a system, how easily they can use it, and what problems they actually need solved.

We also focused on developing a clear and meaningful problem statement. I learned that a well-defined problem statement identifies the user, the challenge, the context, and the purpose behind the solution. This provides a focused direction for research, design decisions, and innovation.

In addition, we had a half-day industry expert session from Bosch focused on project management and mind mapping. The session introduced us to ways of organizing project ideas, breaking complex work into manageable components, and structuring project activities using visual project management approaches.

Overall, Week 5 strengthened my understanding of user-centered design, problem definition, structured thinking, and project planning.

User-Centered Design & Usability

Good design begins with understanding the user.

We explored user needs, user difficulties, usability, accessibility, clear interaction, the user journey, context of use, and human-centered design. Looking at the experience from the user's perspective helps a solution become more intuitive and useful.

Problem Statement Development

A clear problem creates a focused direction for innovation.

We focused on how to develop a problem statement in a structured and meaningful way. A strong problem statement identifies who is experiencing the problem, what the problem is, its context, why it matters, and what need the solution should address.
